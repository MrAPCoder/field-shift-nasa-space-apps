NASA Data & Sources

The simulator runs on daily weather from NASA POWER, saved with the site. Using the app never contacts NASA. The data was downloaded once, checked, and stored with SHA-256 fingerprints.

NASA POWER daily weather (the simulator)

Source: NASA Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER), daily point API, agroclimatology community. Period used by the simulator: 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2025 (1,826 days per farm). The simulator does not use the full 1981–2025 archive.

VariableUnitsHow it is used
PRECTOTCORR precipitationmm/dayWater entering the soil, and precipitation on exposed soil
T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN temperature°CDrying demand (FAO-56 Penman–Monteith); freezing-day flags
RH2M humidity%Drying demand
ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN sunlightMJ/m²/dayDrying demand; sky brightness in the illustration
WS2M wind, PS pressurem/s, kPaDrying demand
GWETROOT root-zone wetness0–1 indexNot used by the simulator or the score (see below)

Representative points (POWER grid cells, not individual fields): Skagit Valley 48.4396° N, 122.3861° W · Yakima Valley 46.2547° N, 119.7285° W · Palouse 46.7609° N, 117.1857° W.

The saved files are public/data/<farm>/soil-weather.json, with source URLs and fingerprints in each file. Missing values are never filled in. If a day is missing a required variable, the simulation stops and says so.

What NASA data is not used for

  • GWETROOT is a modeled, regional root-zone wetness index. It is not a field sensor reading and is not the simulator's soil water. It neither starts nor checks the calculation.
  • SMAP soil moisture (SPL3SMP v9) is unavailable. Access was blocked, so no SMAP data is used. Product page · access record.

Other published sources

  • FAO-56, chapters 2–3: daily Penman–Monteith reference evapotranspiration
  • FAO-56, table 12: crop coefficients for spring wheat, dry peas and potatoes
  • FAO-56, table 17: basal coefficients for the residue adjustment
  • FAO-56, chapter 8: available water, depletion and the water balance
  • FAO-56, chapter 10: approximate organic-mulch adjustment
  • Virginia Tech BSE-194P, table 1: generic soil water storage (sand, loam, clay)

Earlier research tools

The earlier 1981–2025 climate profile, historical replay screen, soil profile and rotation evidence are kept in the research archive. Full methods, audits and verification records are in the project repository under docs/ (start with docs/METHODS.md and docs/NASA_SIMULATION_DATA_AUDIT.md).
